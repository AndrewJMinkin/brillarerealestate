03 / Our story

Built by students.
For what's ahead.

Brillare Scholars began with a simple idea: young people should have the opportunity to understand—and help shape—the world being built around them.

Founded by Andrew Minkin in Chicago, our student-led initiative connects architecture and business through accessible real estate education. We're here to help young learners turn curiosity into practical skills and confidence.

A few things to know

Curiosity welcomed.
Questions, too.

Do I need to know anything about real estate?

No prior real estate knowledge is needed. The program introduces the fundamentals through beginner-friendly lessons.

Is the program really free?

Yes. The self-paced real estate program is free to join.

How does the four-week course work?

The program is designed as a four-week learning experience, with self-paced lessons you can work through on your own schedule.

Will I receive a certificate?

You'll receive a personalized certificate after completing the course and submitting your capstone project.

Can my school host a workshop?

Contact Brillarescholars@gmail.com to discuss a presentation or hands-on workshop at your school or community center.
