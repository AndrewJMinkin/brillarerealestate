Our Services

01

Core Real Estate Program

A self-paced course teaching youth the fundamentals of real estate, development, financial fundamentals in real estate, careers, and more.

Students can also dive deeper with optional bonus modules exploring entrepreneurship, technology, economics, and more.

02

In-Person Presentations & Workshops

Engaging classroom sessions that introduce real estate basics to students through interactive presentations and workshops where they implement what they have learned.

Our workshops let students apply real estate concepts in hands-on projects that emphasize community and environmental impact, and practical problem-solving.

03

Community Initiatives

Partnering with organizations to raise funds and awareness for affordable housing.

Students may take part in meaningful community projects that teach teamwork, problem-solving, and the impact of real estate on communities.

Brillare Scholars

Mission

Our mission is to empower students by providing personalized, high-quality education in real estate. We strive to create a supportive learning environment where young people gain practical skills, confidence, and a deeper understanding of the world around them.

02 / About

About

Brillare Scholars is a Chicago-based youth education initiative led by students, for students, founded by Andrew Minkin, a high school student passionate about bridging the gap between architecture and business through real estate. We offer self-paced learning experiences focused on real estate fundamentals. Our passionate team is dedicated to helping young learners build practical skills through engaging, hands-on lessons designed to inspire confidence and real-world success.
