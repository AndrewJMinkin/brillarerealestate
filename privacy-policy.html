Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 06/28/2025

Brillare Scholars ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, and any other details you provide through our sign-up forms, or contact page.
  • Usage Data: Information on how you interact with the site, collected via cookies or analytics tools (e.g., Wix Analytics), including IP address, browser type, time spent on pages, etc.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Enroll students in our online courses
  • Provide access to the student portal and learning materials
  • Respond to inquiries or requests
  • Share updates or important information related to Brillare Scholars

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely through Wix's built-in tools. We take reasonable precautions to protect your information from loss, misuse, unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration.

4. Sharing Information

We only share information:

  • When legally required (e.g., to comply with law enforcement)
  • With your consent
  • With trusted services we use (e.g., Wix), solely for website and service operation

5. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without parental consent. If we learn that personal data from a child has been collected improperly, we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, please contact us through our website.

7. Use of Cookies

Our site may use cookies to provide a better user experience and collect analytics data. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us using the contact form on our website or at:

brillarescholars@gmail.com
